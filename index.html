Research portfolio

My work links material design with measurable electrochemical and photoelectrochemical performance. The core approach is to create controlled material environments, validate them with complementary characterization, and connect structural evidence to functional outcomes.

Main research · 2024–2026

Vacancy-directed Pt single-atom anchoring in Mo₂TiC₂Tₓ MXene through a two-stage electrochemical Carve-and-Patch strategy.

Key outcome0.50 wt% Pt loading in vacancy-rich MXene, 4.2× the vacancy-poor control

Main research · 2020–2024

Heterojunction Photocatalysts for Environmental Energy Conversion

Visible-light CaBiO₂X/g-C₃N₄ composite photocatalysts for CO₂ reduction and Rh6G degradation.

Key outcome0.0507 h⁻¹ degradation rate constant for CaBi₂O₃Br₂/5 wt% g-C₃N₄

Side project · 2024–2025
