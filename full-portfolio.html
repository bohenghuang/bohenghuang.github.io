Project case studies

Vacancy-directed Pt single-atom anchoring in Mo₂TiC₂Tₓ MXene

2024–2026 · M.S. main research

Developed a two-stage electrochemical Carve-and-Patch workflow that separated vacancy generation from Pt incorporation. The project examines how deliberately engineered Mo-vacancy environments alter Pt anchoring and the HER response.

My workIndependently prepared and electrochemically engineered MXene samples; operated CV/LSV, XRD, Raman, PL and XPS peak fitting. Data interpretation was discussed with the advisor.EvidenceVacancy-rich MXene reached 0.50 wt% Pt loading, compared with 0.12 wt% for the vacancy-poor control (4.2×).Functional resultPt@high-vacancy MXene showed a 215 mV HER onset-potential improvement, versus 123 mV for the low-vacancy counterpart.OutputFirst-author paper in Chemical Communications (2026) and 2026 KCS oral presentation in Jeju, South Korea.

CaBiO₂X/g-C₃N₄ heterojunction photocatalysts

2020–2024 · B.S. main research

Developed visible-light photocatalysts based on CaBiO₂Cl and CaBi₂O₃Br₂ coupled with g-C₃N₄, targeting CO₂ reduction and Rh6G degradation. The project connects heterojunction construction with environmental-energy conversion.

My workMaterials synthesis, photocatalytic testing, characterization and research presentation development across the undergraduate project.Functional resultCaBi₂O₃Br₂/5 wt% g-C₃N₄ reached a 0.0507 h⁻¹ Rh6G degradation rate constant, compared with 0.0182 h⁻¹ for pristine CaBi₂O₃Br₂ (about 2.8×).CO₂ reductionVisible-light product analysis identified hydrocarbons, with CH₄ as the major detected product.OutputPoster presentation at the 2023 Taiwan International Conference on Catalysis.

Visible-light-responsive Mo₂TiC₂Tₓ MXene for HER

2024–2025 · M.S. side project

Investigated delaminated Mo₂TiC₂Tₓ MXene as a visible-light-responsive photoelectrode for hydrogen evolution after a safer NH₄BF₄/H₂SO₄ hydrothermal etching route.

My workTEM characterization and faculty-reviewed data analysis.Functional resultUnder visible light, delaminated Mo₂TiC₂Tₓ delivered 0.512 mA cm⁻² at 0 V vs RHE, a 50% photocurrent increase over the multilayer counterpart.OutputCo-author paper in Journal of Physical Chemistry C (2026).

Theoretical photoelectron spectroscopy of ethylketene

2020–2024 · B.S. side project

Used quantum-chemical calculations to simulate ethylketene photoelectron spectra and clarify the role of molecular normal modes in vibrational structure.

MethodsGaussian 16; M06-2X/APFD; aug-cc-pVTZ; Franck–Condon factors; Duschinsky-effect analysis; harmonic and anharmonic models.OutcomeCalculated spectra were evaluated against experiment through treatment of 27 normal modes.OutputPoster presentation at a 2023 Chemical Society meeting and co-authorship on a Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation paper (2025).
